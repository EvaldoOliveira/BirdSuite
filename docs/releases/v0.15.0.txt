v0.15.0 - Stations map, station ID, station list sync

New
- Stations map (Settings › Stations): every registered station at its current location, green when it answers, red
  at the last position it reported (with the time it was seen), its other locations in yellow with their names. The
  list of the stations sits beside the map (under it on a phone): a checkbox per station (its layer), its state and
  location; a click on a name centres the map there. Refresh asks every station now; Maximize (bottom right) shows the
  map and the list in the whole window, Back or Esc returns. Offline world map without internet.
- Search the network: finds the BirdSuite stations of the local network that are not registered yet (an mDNS
  broadcast, not over the VPN) and adds the chosen ones with their position.
- Sync with a station: reads the list of another registered station and adds the stations it has that are not here.
- Station ID: a station is identified by the MAC of the Pi's own network port (else its own WiFi), never a USB
  adapter's; the Stations table shows its WiFi name (BirdSuite-<ID>) with the MAC under it. The name is an alias: two
  stations may share it (the lists then add the last 6 digits of the ID), and Import replaces the station with the
  same ID.
- Export / Import of the station list carry the last known positions (the newer one wins).
- Danger zone: Clear ALL data and Factory settings run on a screen of their own, with their output, as the update.
- Review player on a phone: the top cells read Detection conf. and Prob. at the location.
- Tools › Defaults › Sharing › ACTIVATE_BIRDDB (false by default) shows or hides the BirdDB-Br section of Settings ›
  Repositories.

Notes
- The BirdDB-Br section is hidden until the upload without an account per station is decided (US-124); the upload
  code is unchanged and a settings form without the section keeps BIRDDB_ENABLED as it is.
- Backup/Restore › Settings points to Settings › Stations for the station list sync.
