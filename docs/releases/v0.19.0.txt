v0.19.0 - external backup of old sounds, smoother review to the end of a list

New
- External backup of old sounds: beside Purge and Keep, Tools › Data offers External backup. The old sounds go to a
  folder on another disk (<folder>/<station>/By_Date/<date>/<species>/) instead of being deleted; their detections stay
  in the database and the spectrograms are drawn again from the audio. Used by the full-disk purge, the per-species
  limit and the daily cap. Choose the destination folder and what to do when the backup is not possible (folder not
  mounted, not writable, full or on the station's own disk): stop the recording (the default) or delete the oldest.
  Station Doctor checks the folder.

Changed
- Validation › Calibrate thresholds: Apply closes the window and says how many species were set.
- Validation: a notice when nothing is left to review in the list shown, at the start and at the end of a review.

Fixed
- Review: going on to the next species no longer opens the page of every species with nothing left to review.
- Review: at the end of the list the player goes back to Validation instead of staying on the species page.

Notes
- The sounds stay on the station until you choose External backup; nothing changes on update.
- The backup needs a folder on another disk with at least 1 GB free.
- Translations: the new texts are in all eleven languages.
